Maison Privée

Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 October 2026

Maison Privée is a private vault for your photos, videos and passwords. It is built so that your content stays on your phone. There is no account, no advertising, and no analytics or tracking of any kind.

What stays on your device

Everything you add to the vault — photos, videos, albums and saved passwords — is encrypted on your phone with a key derived from your PIN, and stored only in the app's private storage. We operate no servers, so your vault content is never sent to us and we cannot see it. Uninstalling the app removes it.

What can leave your device, only if you choose

  • Encrypted backup to your own Google Drive. If you connect Google Drive, the app can save a backup to a hidden folder in your Google account that only this app can access. The backup is encrypted on your phone before it leaves and can be opened only with your recovery key. Google cannot read it, and neither can we. The app requests only the limited drive.appdata permission, which reaches that hidden folder and nothing else in your Drive. You can turn this off or disconnect at any time.
  • Membership purchases. Optional "Privé" membership is sold through Google Play billing and managed with RevenueCat, which processes your purchase and receipt information to confirm and restore your membership. This does not include your vault content. See Google Play's and RevenueCat's privacy policies for how they handle purchase data.

Permissions

  • Photos and videos are chosen through Android's system photo picker, so the app sees only the items you pick and never asks for broad access to your gallery.
  • Fingerprint / biometric unlock, if you enable it, is handled by your phone's secure hardware; no biometric data reaches the app or us.
  • Internet access is used only for the two optional features above (Google Drive backup and membership). With them unused, the app makes no network connections.

Children

Maison Privée is intended for adults and is not directed to children.

Your control and deletion

Your vault lives on your device; uninstalling the app deletes it. Backups you made to Google Drive stay in your Drive until you delete them, from within the app or in Google Drive itself. To end membership, cancel it in your Google Play account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
